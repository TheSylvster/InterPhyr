Zapadiya People's Republic

Member of the Phyrrian Astral Federation.

Political Ideology: Socialism

Economic Ideology: Collectivism

Alignment: Titoism

What Does This Mean?

Socialism

A political and economic system in which the means of production are owned or regulated by the community or state, aiming for a more equal distribution of wealth and resources.

Collectivism

An economic approach that emphasizes group ownership and cooperative control of resources and production over individual private ownership.

Titoism

A form of socialism associated with Josip Broz Tito, marked by worker self-management, decentralized planning, and staying non-aligned with either major bloc in a divided world.
