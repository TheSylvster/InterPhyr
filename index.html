This is the official website of the Phyrrian Astral Federation.

Last updated: 199X

Information

InterPhyr is the official information service of the Phyrrian Astral Federation.

This website provides information about the federation and its participating member nations.

NOTICE: This website is currently under construction.

Goals

  • Encourage cooperation between member nations.
  • Coordinate common projects.
  • Maintain diplomatic relations.
  • Promote mutual development.

Political & Economic Profiles

The following information provides a brief overview of the political and economic systems of Phyrrian Astral Federation member nations.

  • Astralyia Republic - Democratic political system with a mixed-market economy.
  • Zapadiya People's Republic - Socialist political system with a collectivist economy.
  • Kingdom of Rendale - Council-Type Monarchy with a peaceful democratic economy.
