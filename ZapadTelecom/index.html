About ZapadTelecom©

ZapadTelecom© is the internet service and hosting provider responsible for InterPhyr.net, along with dial-up connectivity across the Phyrrian Astral Federation.

Services include web hosting, telephone exchanges, and data centers, all running on infrastructure best described as "adequate."

Ownership

Zapadiyan Peoples Information Ministry

ZapadTelecom© is owned and controlled by the Zapadiyan Peoples Information Ministry, the state body responsible for information services, telecommunications, and public broadcasting within the Zapadiya People's Republic.

As a state-run enterprise, ZapadTelecom©'s operations - including the hosting of InterPhyr.net - are ultimately overseen by the Ministry on behalf of the Zapadiyan People's Republic.
